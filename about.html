Our mission is to take the intimidation out of browsing and replace it with genuine, friendly guidance. BOURBON & WHISKEY

Shawnee's Bourbon & Whiskey Destination

For whiskey lovers across Kansas, Whiskey Barrel is renowned as a premier bourbon and whiskey destination in Shawnee, KS. The world of whiskey is vast, steeped in heritage and distinct regional traditions, and our shelves celebrate every facet of this legendary spirit. American whiskey enthusiasts will discover a diverse array of Kentucky straight bourbons, small-batch releases, high-rye expressions, and single-barrel selections known for their rich notes of toasted oak, caramel, vanilla, and warm spice. From beloved daily sippers to coveted bottles from iconic distilleries like Buffalo Trace, Four Roses, Elijah Craig, Maker's Mark, and Woodford Reserve, our selection is continuously refreshed to surprise and delight collectors.

Beyond traditional American bourbon, our whiskey collection spans the globe. Explore the peated complexity and coastal elegance of single malt and blended Scotch whiskies from Islay, Speyside, and the Scottish Highlands. Discover the triple-distilled smoothness of authentic Irish whiskeys, crisp Canadian blended whiskies, and the meticulous harmony of Japanese whiskies. If you enjoy classic cocktails like the Old Fashioned or Manhattan, our bold rye whiskeys offer the spicy punch and dry complexity that elevate any home bar. Our knowledgeable team is always available to discuss flavor notes, mash bills, and aging profiles, helping you choose the perfect pour.

FINE WINE CELLAR

Curated Wine for Every Palate & Occasion

A great liquor store should be an inspiring place to explore wine, and Whiskey Barrel delivers with a vibrant, accessible wine selection right here in Shawnee. Wine connects people across celebrations, intimate meals, and casual gatherings. We feature a broad spectrum of varietals, regions, and styles designed to accommodate every taste and budget, from approachable weekday table wines to celebratory cellar-worthy vintages.

Red wine enthusiasts will appreciate our handpicked California Cabernet Sauvignons, expressive Willamette Valley Pinot Noirs, robust Argentine Malbecs, and rich Italian red blends. For those who prefer white wine, our coolers and shelves feature crisp New Zealand Sauvignon Blancs, buttery California Chardonnays, floral Pinot Grigios, and aromatic German and domestic Moscatos. We also showcase refreshing dry rosés and effervescent sparkling wines, including classic Champagne, Italian Prosecco, and sparkling cavas that bring celebratory sparkle to anniversaries, birthdays, and holiday dinners. If you are preparing a dinner menu or planning an event in Shawnee, our staff is happy to offer personalized food pairing recommendations.

CRAFT BEER & SELTZERS

Kansas City Craft Beer & Chilled Seltzers

Kansas City has one of the most vibrant, respected craft brewing cultures in the country, and Whiskey Barrel proudly showcases local Midwest beers alongside top national and international favorites. We maintain a large walk-in cooler stocked with chilled six-packs, four-packs, and single cans, ensuring your craft beer is ice-cold and brewery-fresh when you walk out our doors.

Explore hop-forward India Pale Ales (IPAs), juicy hazy brews, rich imperial stouts, crisp pilsners, refreshing wheat beers, and seasonal ales crafted by local and regional brewmasters. From hometown legends like Boulevard Brewing to experimental microbrews, our beer selection reflects the creative spirit of our region. For those who prefer gluten-free alternatives or lighter beverage options, we stock an assortment of hard ciders, ready-to-drink canned cocktails, and premium real-fruit hard seltzers such as High Noon, White Claw, and Truly. Whether you are hosting a Kansas City Chiefs game day tailgate, firing up the backyard barbecue, or heading to a weekend lake trip, we have your cooler covered.

TEQUILA & PREMIUM SPIRITS

100% Blue Agave Tequila & Artisanal Spirits

Tequila and agave spirits have experienced an unprecedented renaissance, and Whiskey Barrel is proud to present a premier selection of 100% blue Weber agave tequilas in Shawnee, KS. Discover crisp, unaged Blanco tequilas that highlight the bright herbal and citrus sweetness of pure agave—perfect for fresh margaritas and palomas. Experience the subtle vanilla and honey notes of barrel-rested Reposados, or indulge in the complex, velvet sophistication of Añejos and Extra Añejos aged in charred oak casks for contemplative neat sipping. For lovers of smoky depth, our artisanal mezcals deliver earthy, hand-roasted character distilled using ancestral methods in Oaxaca.

Our spirits selection extends well beyond agave. Stock your home cocktail bar with ultra-clean, multiple-distilled vodkas, aromatic London Dry and botanical gins, spiced and aged Caribbean rums, brandies, and artisan liqueurs. Whether you are crafting a martini, mixing a tiki punch, or preparing holiday eggnog, you will find the spirits and specialty cordials you need under one roof.
